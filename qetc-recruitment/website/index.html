Admission & visa guidance

How the process usually works

  1. Enquiry & shortlist. We discuss your goals, qualifications, budget and preferred intake, and shortlist suitable courses.
  2. University application. You submit your application with genuine documents. I help you make sure it is complete.
  3. Offer letter. The university decides on your application and, if successful, issues an offer.
  4. Student pass application. International students need a student pass. Applications are normally submitted through Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS), the official agency, with your university.
  5. Prepare to travel. Once approved, we coordinate accommodation, airport pickup and your arrival plan.
  6. Arrival & registration. You complete arrival formalities, any required medical screening and university registration.

I provide guidance and coordination, not legal immigration advice. Visa decisions are made by the Malaysian authorities. Always follow the official instructions from your university and EMGS.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can you guarantee my admission or visa?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee admission or a visa. Universities make admission decisions and the Malaysian authorities decide on student passes. I help you choose suitable options and submit a complete, genuine application.

Are your services free?

Sending an enquiry and talking to me costs nothing and puts you under no obligation. QETC publishes service packages, and some services carry a charge. I’ll tell you exactly what applies to you, and any price will be confirmed in writing, before you decide.

Which countries do you accept students from?

I welcome enquiries from every country. Eligibility can depend on your nationality, qualifications and the university, so I confirm your options personally.

Do I still have to do any paperwork?

Yes – you provide genuine documents, complete the required applications and follow immigration procedures. I make the process clearer and coordinate the arrangements so you are not doing it alone.

What documents will I need?

Usually academic certificates and transcripts, a passport and often proof of English. The exact list depends on the university. Please don’t send documents through the website or chat – I’ll explain the secure, official way when you’re ready to apply.

Do I need IELTS?

Many universities ask for proof of English such as IELTS or an equivalent, unless you qualify for an exemption. I’ll check the requirement for your chosen course.

Can you help with scholarships?

Yes, I can guide you to scholarships and merit discounts you may qualify for. They are competitive and never guaranteed.

Can you arrange accommodation and airport pickup?

Yes, accommodation assistance and airport pickup can be arranged through QETC. Rent is paid separately, and any service charge is confirmed before you travel.

Am I talking to a real person?

The chat on this site is an automated virtual assistant that collects your enquiry. Ismail Hassan, a real person, reads every enquiry and replies personally.

How is my personal data used?

Only to respond to your enquiry and, if you choose to proceed, to coordinate your application with QETC Malaysia. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time – see the privacy notice.
