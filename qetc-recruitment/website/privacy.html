Privacy notice

Your privacy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who is responsible

This website is run by Ismail Hassan, an international student recruitment consultant and QETC Malaysia recruitment partner (“I”, “me”). I am responsible for the enquiry information you send through this website.

What I collect

Only what you choose to give me in the enquiry form or chat: your name, country, contact details (email and/or WhatsApp number), qualification, study interests, preferred intake, budget range, the services you’re interested in, and any message you write. I do not ask for passport numbers, bank details or card details through this website.

Why I use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and give you guidance about studying in Malaysia.
  • If you decide to proceed, to coordinate your application and arrangements with QETC Malaysia and, through them, the university you choose.
  • To keep a simple record of your enquiry and follow-ups.

I only store your enquiry if you give consent. I will not send you marketing messages you didn’t ask for and I never sell your information.

Prioritising enquiries

A simple automatic scoring system (based on how complete your enquiry is, e.g. whether you’ve chosen a course and intake) helps me see which enquiries to answer first. It has no effect on any admission or visa decision, and every enquiry is read by me personally.

Who can see it

Me. Your details are stored in my private Google account (Google Sheets), so Google acts as my service provider. If you decide to proceed with an application, I share the details needed with QETC Malaysia and the university you apply to.

Public posts

I look at public online discussions where people ask about studying abroad, so I can offer helpful public answers. I only keep a link to and a short excerpt of the public post. I do not collect usernames, email addresses or phone numbers from public posts, and I don’t send unsolicited private messages.

How long I keep it

Until you ask me to delete it, or at most 24 months after our last contact, after which I delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time. To delete it yourself, use the form below with the reference number you received after your enquiry (it starts with “QM-”). You can also contact me directly.

Delete my data

If you only gave a WhatsApp number, message me on WhatsApp and I will delete your enquiry manually.

Important

I provide guidance and coordination, not legal or immigration advice. Admission decisions are made by universities and student pass decisions by the Malaysian authorities; they are never guaranteed. For official information, see Education Malaysia Global Services (EMGS).
