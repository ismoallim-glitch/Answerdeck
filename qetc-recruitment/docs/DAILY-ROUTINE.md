# Your daily routine (about 20 minutes)

The system finds and sorts; you build the relationships.

## When an alert email arrives

- **🎓 NEW QUALIFIED STUDENT!** Contact the student personally within 24 hours. The email has a one-tap WhatsApp link. Then change their **Status** in the Students sheet (for example to `REFERRED TO QETC`).
- **New student enquiry:** reply within 1–2 working days and ask for whatever the score explanation says is missing.
- **🔎 Promising student post:** open the link. If the community allows it and the person asked for advice, post a helpful **public** reply. A draft is in the *Discovered Opportunities* sheet; edit it so it sounds like you. Then set *Review Status* to `REPLIED PUBLICLY` or `IGNORED`.
- **⚠️ Recruitment system problem:** look at *System Log*. Usually it is one source failing, and the system retries on its own.

## Every morning (daily summary email)

1. Work through **FOLLOW-UPS DUE**.
2. Glance at **NEW PUBLIC POSTS** and reply to the best 2–3.
3. If someone you replied to publicly then contacts you or fills in the form, select their row in *Discovered Opportunities* and use **🎓 Recruitment → Convert selected post → student lead**. Add their details only if they gave them to you voluntarily.

## Weekly

- Open the private dashboard (link in *System Status*). Check conversion rates and which countries and platforms work best, then add Google Alerts for what works.
- Update statuses after talking to QETC: APPLICATION SUBMITTED, ADMISSION OFFERED, ENROLLED.
- When QETC confirms commission eligibility or pays, update *Commission (Private)*.

## Rules to keep

- Never promise admission, visas, or that everything is free.
- Don't DM people who didn't ask. Public replies only, and follow each community's rules on self-promotion.
- Don't collect passport scans or bank details over WhatsApp or email unless it's through the official secure process QETC or the university gives you.
- If a student asks for deletion, use **🎓 Recruitment → Delete a student's data**.
