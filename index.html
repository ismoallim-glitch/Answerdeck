Which phone number will customers call?

Does it say it’s an AI?

Yes. It introduces itself as an AI assistant and says so if asked.

What if there’s an emergency?

You tell us what counts as urgent. Urgent calls are transferred to your phone, and if the transfer doesn’t connect, the caller’s details are taken and flagged. It only uses the information you give it and doesn’t give advice.

How does the free trial work?

You get 2 weeks free on your real line. No subscription payment is taken during the trial. Keep it and you’re charged after; cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before your next monthly payment. There’s no long-term contract.

How do I pay?

Secure checkout by Stripe, using major credit and debit cards.

Calls can be recorded so you can review them. We’ll agree the call notice wording with you, and you can ask for recordings to be turned off.
